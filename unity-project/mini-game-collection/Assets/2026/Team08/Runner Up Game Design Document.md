# 1. Project Vision
Runner Up is a competitive racing game where 2 Players race against each other and also have to dodge obstacles. Players have 30 seconds to make it to the farthest. Whoever makes it furthest is declared the winner.
# 2. Goal
Can we instill competitiveness amongst 2 Players by testing their reflexes against each other?
# 3. Game Mechanics
The Players will have the ability to Move, Jump, and to Send Hazards to the other Player.

Controls (per player):
- One 8-way joystick pushed to the right for movement
- 2 buttons
	- Primary action = Jump
	- Secondary action = Attack (sending Hazards to other Player's track)
# 4. Game World
The Game World for both Players will take place on the same Arcade Screen. It will consist of 2 Tracks, the Top Track is for Player 1, and the Bottom Track is for Player 2. Both Tracks will have a colour specific to the Player as well. Player 1's Track is Green while Player 2's Track is purple.
# 5. Player Characters
Both Players' Characters are 2D Sprite Animals sourced by DesertShooter and provided by MegaSuperAwesome Games Co.

Player 1 plays as a Blackish Grey Mouse.

Player 2 plays as a Light Brown Beaver.
# 6. Story
The Annual Death Race between Mouseopolis and BeaverTown has arrived! Both Cities have chosen their Champions after a month of specially selected athletes training their butts off and it is now time to show off the fruits of their training!
# 7. Game Interface
The Game's Interface will display Input Prompts to inform the Player of what controls they should be using such as an Icon showing the Player to push the Joystick to the right or pressing the Primary Action button to Jump when a Hazard gets close.
# 8. Audio
The Audio will be provided by MegaSuperAwesome Games Co.

Audio present in the game as music in the Title Screen and In-Game, and sound effects for Player Movement, Player Jumping, and Player Hurt.

Team08 has chosen to use Juhani Junkala's Retro Game Music Pack Level 2 for the Title Screen and Fast Fight Battle Music Looped for the In-Game music.

Team08 has chosen to use DesertShooter's move, jump, and hurt sfx to indicate Player Movement, Player Jump, and Player Hurt.
# 9. Assets
Assets will be provided by MegaSuperAwesome Games Co.

Team08 has chosen to use DesertShooter, SpaceShooterRedux, and InputPrompts sprites. To get more into the specifics, the Game Background, Player 1, Player 2, and the Players' Tracks will use DesertShooter sprites, the Hazards will use SpaceShooterRedux meteor sprites, and the InputPrompts sprites will be used to indicate controls to the Players.
